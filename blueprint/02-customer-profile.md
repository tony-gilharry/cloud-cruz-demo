# 02 Customer profile

**Cloud Cruz is built first for mid-market software companies that must ship AI inside their own product this year. Partners follow the customer, so this profile decides which partners Cloud Cruz recruits.** (Decision 14)

> All of this is synthetic. Cloud Cruz and its customers are invented.

## Primary customer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | B2B software companies, roughly 200 to 2,000 employees |
| **Where** | The United States, the Americas and English-speaking companies, to start (decision 67) |
| **What they're doing** | Shipping AI features and agents inside their own product |
| **Buyer** | CTO or VP Engineering |
| **Trigger** | A "ship AI this year" mandate from the board or CEO |
| **Plan they buy** | Premium ($12K to $50K ACV), growing into Enterprise ($50K+) |
| **Partners pulled in** | Builder and Specialist partners to build it; ISVs whose products they already run |

**How you recognize them** (the markers a partner development rep or a partner can spot):
- They already sell a product with paying customers, and AI is now on the roadmap, not in a lab.
- Their product data lives in several places (an app database, a warehouse, a few SaaS tools) and nobody owns joining it.
- Their data and ML team is small, often a handful of people, so the work outruns the team.
- They care about time to ship more than about building infrastructure themselves.

## Secondary customer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Enterprise data teams (5,000+ employees) |
| **Buyer** | Chief Data Officer or Head of Data |
| **Trigger** | Consolidating data platforms and putting agents on company data |
| **Plan** | Enterprise, annual or multi-year |
| **How Cloud Cruz reaches them** | Through Scale and Anchor partners (consultancies and holding-company data units) and cloud marketplaces, not a large direct sales force |

## Not a fit (yet)

- **Pre-product startups:** no customers, no data, no mandate. Self-serve is enough.
- **Teams set on building their own platform:** they want parts, not a platform.

Naming who isn't a fit saves partners time: they stop chasing deals that won't go live.

## Why this matters for partners

| Customer marker | Partner who serves it | Segment |
|---|---|---|
| Needs hands to ship AI fast | Boutique AI studios and consultancies | Builder |
| Needs data work done right | Independent digital and data agencies | Specialist |
| Runs software that could embed Cloud Cruz | ISVs on the Technology path | Technology |
| Enterprise data platform decision | Consultancy digital arms, holding-company data units | Scale, Anchor |

The customer's time and money are the test: a partner is worth recruiting when it gets this customer live faster and cheaper than they could alone. That is also what the north star counts: customers live through partners. (Decision 57)
