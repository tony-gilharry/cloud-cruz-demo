# 01 Scenario

**Cloud Cruz is the data and AI platform your apps and agents run on. It has no partner program. This Blueprint designs one from zero.** (Decision 13)

> All of this is synthetic. Cloud Cruz, its partners, customers and numbers are invented.

## What Cloud Cruz sells

A platform that companies build their apps and AI agents on, and that software vendors embed in their own products. Three plans (decision 60):

| Plan | Who | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Self-serve | Teams starting out, card on file | Under $12K a year |
| Premium | Growing companies, annual contract | $12K to $50K ACV |
| Enterprise | Sales-led, annual or multi-year | $50K+ ACV |

## Who buys it

Mid-market B2B software companies, roughly 200 to 2,000 employees, shipping AI features and agents inside their own products. The buyer is the CTO or VP Engineering. The trigger is a mandate to ship AI this year. Enterprise data teams are the second market. (Decision 14; details in `02-customer-profile.md`.)

## Why partners, and why now

- **Customers need hands.** A mid-market software team with a "ship AI this year" mandate rarely has the data engineers to do it alone. Agencies and boutique AI consultancies build it with them.
- **Software vendors are distribution.** An ISV that embeds Cloud Cruz brings it to every one of its customers.
- **Enterprise runs through the firms it already trusts.** Consultancies and cloud marketplaces are how Cloud Cruz reaches large data teams without a large sales force.

A partner program turns those three facts into a repeatable motion instead of a series of one-off favors.

## The one number the program answers to

**Customers live through partners:** the count of Cloud Cruz customers in production with a partner as the sourcing partner, partner of record or implementer, with time to live beside it. Revenue, ROI and activation are supporting metrics. It passes both tests: one plain number, and it measures customers getting value. (Decision 57)

## Year-1 targets (by Mar 31, 2028)

Outcomes, not partner counts, on a partner team that stays at 9 people all year (decisions 65, 66):

| Target | Number |
|---|---|
| **Customers live through partners** (north star) | **400** |
| Partners activated (a first live joint customer) | 250 |
| Partners activated within 90 days of signing | 40% |
| Customers live per partner-team member | 44+ |
| Partner questions answered by the Support Hub, no ticket needed | 60% |
| Long-tail onboarding completed with no human touch | 70% |

About 1,000 partners signed is an input to these outcomes, not a goal. Scale comes from systems, not headcount: automation and AI do the routine work, and a person approves anything that changes state. The targets are reviewed Jan 1 and Jul 1. (How they were chosen: [`decision-briefs/2026-09-25-year-one-targets.md`](decision-briefs/2026-09-25-year-one-targets.md).)
