# Research and sources

**The outside numbers behind the case study, with who published them, when, how they were gathered, and what to keep in mind. Every figure was checked on the page it links to.**

> Cloud Cruz is fictional; these sources describe the real market it is designed for.

## Partner programs need rework

| Finding | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nearly half of B2B organizations say their partner program is not profitable by tier level. Two-thirds plan to rearchitect, change or significantly update their program within 12 months. | [Forrester, Modernizing B2B Partner Programs](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/modernizing-b2b-partner-programs-for-the-next-era-of-partner-ecosystem-growth/), Sep 2026 | From the report *The State of B2B Partner Programs, 2026*. Checked on Forrester's summary; the full report is paywalled. |
| Only 36% of large organizations consistently measure partner performance. 71% have trouble aligning partners on goals. 83% plan to expand their partner network in the next 1 to 3 years. | [KPMG, Build value with the right partner ecosystem](https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2025/build-value-with-the-right-partner-ecosystem.html), 2026 | Survey of 258 senior leaders at large organizations. |

Why it matters here: tiers built on counts and customer results (04 Economics), and a Measurement Layer that answers what partners sourced, influenced and cost (build 2).

## Partners move revenue

| Finding | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Two-thirds of companies surveyed expect partner-influenced revenue growth to beat last year's. 67% plan for indirect revenue to grow above or significantly above last year's. | [Forrester, The State of Partner Ecosystems in 2025](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-state-of-partner-ecosystems-2025/), Jul 2025 | Forrester's Partner Ecosystem Marketing Survey. |
| The average lift in win rate when partners are involved in deals is 11.7%. | [Crossbeam, data on partners in deals](https://insider.crossbeam.com/entry/new-data-involving-partners-in-deals-increases-win-rate-for-nearly-every-ecosystem-size-and-type), Nov 2024 | Vendor network data across thousands of companies, as of Oct 28, 2024. The page doesn't say whether the lift is in percentage points or relative. |
| 68% of companies report higher close rates when partners are involved. 64% say more than half of their new customers come through partner-influenced or co-sold deals. | [Bridge Partners and Partnership Leaders, Ecosystem Compass Report 2025](https://www.bridge.partners/insights/new-report-shows-how-high-performing-partner-programs-are-generating-growth-in-2025), Feb 2025 | Checked on the summary page; the full report requires a download. |
| 74% of B2B SaaS leaders see partners as essential to customer satisfaction, expansion and retention. 69% plan to increase partnership investment. | [PartnerStack and Wynter, State of Partnerships in GTM 2026](https://partnerstack.com/articles/state-of-partnerships-gtm-2026-report-press-release), Sep 2025 | Survey of 100 senior leaders at B2B SaaS companies with $50M+ revenue. Vendor press release; small sample. |

Why it matters here: co-selling with account executives from Select tier (05 Lifecycle), and two partners credited on one deal (08 Salesforce setup).

## Ecosystems are being rebuilt for AI

| Finding | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 78% of executives agree that digital ecosystems will need to be built for AI agents as much as for humans over the next 3 to 5 years. | [Accenture, Technology Vision 2025](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/insights/technology/technology-trends-2025), Jan 2025 | More than 4,000 executives in 21 industries and 28 countries, surveyed Oct to Dec 2024. |
