## AWS Partner Network

URLs: https://aws.amazon.com/partners/ , https://aws.amazon.com/partners/paths/ , https://aws.amazon.com/partners/services-tiers/ , https://aws.amazon.com/partners/programs/

- **Paths:** Software, Hardware, Services, Training, Distribution. Software validates through the Foundational Technical Review; Services partners earn Services Partner Tiers.
- **Services tiers:**

  | | Select | Advanced | Premier |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Accredited individuals | 4 | 8 | 20 |
  | Technical certified | 2 | 6 (3 Pro/Specialty) | 25 (10 Pro/Specialty) |
  | Launched opportunities | 3 (MRR >= $1,500) | 20 (MRR >= $10,000) | 50 (MRR >= $50,000) |
  | Other | none | Partner Business Plan | Business Plan, Executive Business Review, 3 Competencies, sustained 6+ months |
  | Fee | $2,500/yr | $2,500/yr | $2,500/yr |

- **Motions:** co-sell through APN Customer Engagements (partners must become "ACE eligible" before receiving AWS referrals: https://aws.amazon.com/partners/programs/ace/); resale (Solution Provider, Distribution); AWS Marketplace and Channel Partner Private Offers; MDF, opportunity acceleration funds, Migration Acceleration Program.
- **Validation:** AWS Specializations (Competency, Service Delivery, Service Ready) need customer references and technical validation; AWS cites Canalys: "87% of customers highlight AWS Specializations as a top three criteria for Partner selection" (https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/apn/aws-specialization-program-2025-enhancements-and-looking-ahead/).
- **Dated:** Jan 12, 2026: specialization badges in Marketplace search; an automated validation agent cut AI Competency application time "by up to 70%" (same blog). Effective Jan 1, 2026: new Customer Incentive and Partner Growth Incentive; deal registration added to Private Pricing Resell (https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/apn/updates-to-aws-channel-programs-to-drive-growth-in-2026/).

## Google Cloud Partner Network (replaced Partner Advantage)

URLs: https://cloud.google.com/partners , https://partners.cloud.google.com/learn , https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/partners/introducing-google-cloud-partner-network

- **Paths:** "Co-sell and Services Partner Paths" carry tiers; ISVs sit on a technology or "Build" model **[secondary]** (https://flashdba.com/hyperscaler-gtm/co-sell/google-cloud/).
- **Tiers:** Select ("foundational knowledge and successful client engagements"), Premier ("significant investment in certified technical resources"), Diamond ("a robust portfolio of large-scale, complex customer deployments"). Determined by **Capacity** (certifications, sales credentials) and **Capability** (contributions to validated closed/won opportunities). **[secondary]** Reported co-sell ACV thresholds: Select $250K, Premier $2M, Diamond $20M (https://www.crn.com.au/news/2026/cloud/google-partner-program-tiering-system-changes).
- **Competencies** replace specializations, at two levels, in Product, Solution and Industry categories.
- **Scoring:** "an AI-driven engine" that will "automatically apply every successful customer engagement toward a partner's progress".
- **Dated:** announced Dec 16, 2025; rolled out Q1 2026 with a six-month transition window; moved from 2 tiers to 3.

## Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program

URLs: https://partner.microsoft.com/en-us/partnership , https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/partner-capability-score

- **Types and paths:** free enrollment; services partners earn **Solutions Partner designations**; software companies earn **Solutions Partner with certified software** designations (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/referrals/solutions-partner-certified-software-designations-introduction); CSP resale; distributors; Azure Expert MSP.
- **Partner Capability Score:** "a composite score that quantifies the performance of a partner in three categories": Performance (net customer adds), Skilling (certifications), Customer success (usage growth, deployments). Qualify with "at least 70 points" of 100 and "every metric... greater than zero points"; Enterprise and SMB tracks.
- **Motions:** CSP resale, co-sell (including Azure IP co-sell), Marketplace, incentives, SPARK propensity data that routes high-propensity accounts to "co-sell-ready partners" (https://partner.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/article/new-benefits-and-resources-for-partners-fy26).
- **Dated:** FY26 consolidated six solution areas into three (AI Business Solutions, Cloud & AI Platforms, Security). July 2026: SMB-track ACR threshold removed; specialization certifications moving to AI-focused ones; "Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace" announced for September 2026 (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-july).

- **OpenAI Partner Network**, announced mid-June 2026: $150M ecosystem investment, target of "300,000 certified consultants by end of 2026" **[secondary]** (https://www.channelinsider.com/news-and-trends/openai-debuts-partner-network-backed-by-150m-investment/).
- **Frontier Alliances**, Feb 23, 2026: multi-year deals with McKinsey, BCG, Accenture and Capgemini **[secondary]** (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/23/open-ai-consulting-accenture-boston-capgemini-mckinsey-frontier.html).

Specializations: Codex, cybersecurity, AI agents.
- Not public: fees, marketplace or resale terms, MDF.

## Snowflake Partner Network

URLs: https://www.snowflake.com/en/why-snowflake/partners/ (plus /service-partners/, /product-partners/, /snowflake-ready/)

- **Types:** AI Data Cloud Services Partners, AI Data Cloud Product Partners (ISVs), Cloud Partners, Enterprise Technology Partners.
- **Tiers:** Registered (free), then Select, Premier, Elite, each paid tier with an annual fee (amounts not public). Services: "Your tier reflects your certifications, closed pipeline, deal registrations and customer success stories." Product: integration maturity, Marketplace presence and "the number of joint customers."
- **Snowflake Ready validation:** Select or higher, native connector live 6+ months, works on AWS, Azure and GCP, 10 production customers, 2 public references, 1 case study.
- **Motions:** co-sell with Premier and Elite aligned to field sales; Snowflake Marketplace with capacity drawdown against committed spend; referral rebates, MDF, deal registration.
- **Dated:** Sept 9, 2026, effective Nov 2, 2026: services partners standardize on a foundational accreditation; Elite Product Partners must embed Cortex AI; regional tier metrics; partner investment "more than doubled since FY 2024" (https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/snowflake-partner-network-agentic-enterprise/).

## Anthropic: Claude Partner Network

URLs: https://claude.com/partners , https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network , https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub

- "Any organization that is bringing Claude to market is eligible to join"; membership is free.
- **Services Track** (entry as Registered):

  | Tier | Certified individuals | Deployed joint customers | Public stories | Other |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Select | >= 10 | >= 2 in production (trailing 12 months) | >= 1 | none |
  | Preferred | >= 100 | >= 15 | >= 3 | none |
  | Global Premier | >= 1,000 | >= 100 across 3+ regions | >= 15 | Joint business plan, named executive sponsors |

- **Scorecard and cadence:** "Every firm's dashboard shows the same numbers we see, and we verify standing every quarter." Promotions Jan 1 and Jul 1; demotions once a year after 90 days' notice. A certified individual needs an active certification and Claude use in the past 90 days.
- Not public: fees, resale or marketplace terms, MDF amounts, an ISV tier track.

## Synthesis

**Common building blocks, scaled down for a 0-to-1 program**

| Block | What the majors do | Scaled-down option for Cloud Cruz |
|---|---|---|
| Paths by business model | AWS 5 paths; Google co-sell/services plus ISV; Microsoft services vs certified software; Snowflake services/product/cloud/tech | Two paths: **Services** (agency, SI, reseller) and **Technology** (ISV, tech). Partner type stays a field |
| Free entry tier | Registered (AWS, Snowflake, Anthropic); free enrollment (Microsoft) | Free entry tier gated by an agreement and a named contact |
| Tiers on skills, customers, revenue | AWS certs + launched opps + MRR; Anthropic certified people + deployed customers + stories; Google capacity + capability | Count-based: certified people, live joint customers, one public reference; add sourced revenue once data exists; top tier invite-only |
| Validated capability | Specializations, Competencies, certified software, Snowflake Ready | Defer; at most one or two badges with a reference plus technical review |
| Certification | Required at every tier everywhere | One foundations and one technical certification; count only active holders |
| Co-sell / deal registration | ACE, Google co-sell, Microsoft co-sell and SPARK, Snowflake deal reg | One deal registration form, one approval step; sourced and influenced recorded separately |
| Marketplace | Private offers at all hyperscalers and Snowflake | Skip at launch; a public partner directory instead |
| Funding | MDF, MAP, opportunity funds | One small discretionary co-marketing pool plus referral fees |
| Transparent scorecard | Microsoft PCS, Anthropic Partner Hub, Google automated tracking | Each partner sees tier criteria against actuals, refreshed from the system of record |
| Predictable cadence | Anthropic semiannual promotion, annual demotion with notice | Semiannual promotion review, annual demotion review with notice |

**Lifecycle mapped to the stages, Acquire to Optimize** (Stage 0, Develop, was added later in decision 34)

| Stage | AWS | Google | Microsoft | OpenAI | Snowflake | Anthropic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acquire | Free registration | Portal application | Free enrollment | Application; founding partners invited | Free Registered tier | Free application |
| Onboard | Choose a path | Choose a path | Company and credential validation | Not public | Choose a type | Registered, commit to certified practitioners |
| Activate | First ACE opportunity; FTR for software | First co-sell opportunity | First customer adds | Not public | First deal registration or listing | First production deployment |
| Enable | Accreditations, certifications | Certifications, sales credentials | Skilling | Certification push | Accreditation | Partner Academy, Certified Architect |
| Hand off / Co-sell | ACE, then referrals once ACE eligible | Co-sell, influenced opportunities | Co-sell, SPARK routing | Co-sell, FDE pilot | Field alignment at Premier/Elite | Co-selling, referral credit |
| Maintain | Sustained tier, fee, specialization renewal | Automated tracking | Rolling qualification window | Not public | Annual fee | Quarterly verification, annual demotion check |
| Optimize | Specializations, MSP | Competency, Advanced, Diamond | Specializations, certified software | Specializations, Elite | Snowflake Ready, Elite | Preferred, Global Premier |

**Pattern [inferred]:** every program ties Activate to a first validated customer outcome and gates Hand off on proven capability; Optimize is a second validation layer on top of tier.

**Too heavy for 0-to-1:** consumption and ACV thresholds need telemetry a new program lacks; stacked validation layers need audit staff; marketplaces need billing infrastructure; GSI-sized thresholds; frequent restructures; formula-based funding needs finance operations; paid tiers slow early recruiting.

**Closest analog:** Anthropic's Services Track (a 2026 launch with a public, count-based, three-number ladder, a transparent dashboard and a predictable review calendar). **Best scorecard model:** Microsoft's Partner Capability Score ("at least 70 points" with no zero metric).
