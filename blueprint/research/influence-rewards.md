# Rewarding partner influence

Research for decision 94, Oct 2, 2026. Public sources only. Influence means a partner co-sold on a deal Cloud Cruz's own sales team sourced.

## What real programs do

| Program | How influence or co-sell is rewarded | Source |
|---|---|---|
| HubSpot | No revenue share when a HubSpot rep brings the partner in; it still counts toward tier. Cash only through a gated upmarket referral program (top tier, accreditation, deal-size floor). Sourced deals pay 20% for up to 3 years | https://www.hubspot.com/hubspot-partner-program-sales-rules |
| Google Cloud Partner Network | Pre-sales influence and post-sales support on won deals count toward tier and competency progress (tier credit, not cash) | https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/partners/introducing-google-cloud-partner-network/ |
| Microsoft Azure Accelerate | Funds partner assessment and deployment work, customer credits and free Microsoft experts, whoever sourced the deal | https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/introducing-azure-accelerate-fueling-transformation-with-experts-and-investments-across-your-cloud-and-ai-journey/ |
| Databricks Brickbuilder | Source rewards, early-adoption SPIFFs and consumption rebates; MDF by tier. Whether influence alone is paid is not stated | https://www.databricks.com/blog/building-whats-next-together-introducing-brickbuilder-partner-network-agentic-ai-era |
| Salesforce | Incentives for pre-sales activity and post-sales implementation subsidies tied to consumption; amounts not public | https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/rewarding-value-through-new-salesforce-partner-program/ |
| Snowflake | A co-sell and referral program plus services funding. Percentages seen in third-party summaries are not verified on Snowflake's own pages, so they are not used | https://www.snowflake.com/webinars/customer-webinars/snowflake-partner-network-fy25-enhancements-to-co-sell-referral-programs-2024-01-16/ |
| Atlassian | Growth rebates and prioritized lead distribution for specialized partners; basis not public | https://www.atlassian.com/partners/join |
| Okta | Tiers set on influenced revenue (secondary source: an Okta leader quoted by Crossbeam) | https://insider.crossbeam.com/entry/partner-tiers-cheat-sheet |

**Pattern:** most large programs pay cash only on sourced deals and reward influence with tier credit, funded services work or gated programs, which avoids paying twice next to the rep's credit.

## Options considered

| Option | Cost | Risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Services routing back: the influencing partner gets first offer on the implementation | No cash | AE favoritism; the customer can still choose | Adopted |
| B. Implementation package on influenced Enterprise deals | Capped per deal | Low: logged, approved hours | Adopted |
| C. Flat influence SPIFF, AE-confirmed | Fixed | Tagging deals to get paid; a claims step | Held |
| D. Platform credits per influenced win | Low | Little perceived value; needs expiry | Held |
| E. Tier-credit multiplier for influence | Indirect | Inflated tiers | Held |
| F. Gated cash for top tiers | Grows with revenue | Reintroduces double payment | Rejected |
