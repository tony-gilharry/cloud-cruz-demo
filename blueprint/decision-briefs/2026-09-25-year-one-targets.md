# Decision brief: Year-1 targets on a flat team

A simulated leadership room (fictional role archetypes from Tony's Virtual Leadership Team method). Tony Gilharry is Driver and made the final decision. Lens for every seat: will this save time, will this save money, is it the simple thing that works, are we moving with intent, should we think again, does it adapt to the market?

## Decision

What Cloud Cruz's partner program commits to by Mar 31, 2028, on a flat partner team.

## Context and evidence

Cloud Cruz launches its program Apr 1, 2027 after a 90-day beta (decision 15). North star: customers live through partners (decision 57). The implementation-package budget is $100,000 for the beta, about 57 packages (decision 61).

## Options

| Option | Benefits | Costs / risks |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative, about 500 signed | Easy to defend | Quieter story, smaller dataset |
| Base, about 1,000 signed | Matches a real, proven pace | Needs a large lead funnel |
| Stretch, about 1,500 signed | Biggest numbers | Hard to justify for a new program |

## Specialist positions (round 1)

- **VP Global Partnerships:** signed partners is a vanity metric; commit to outcomes.
- **GTM Analytics:** measure activation by monthly cohort, or a large signed count distorts the rate.
- **Finance:** most customers live must arrive without subsidy; the package budget funds enterprise go-lives only; no double counting.
- **GTM Operations:** human attention caps at 60 to 80 managed partners; the signed count should follow capacity.
- **Enablement:** 250 activated partners means about 1,000 accredited people; feasible with free self-paced L1 plus weekly cohorts.
- **Partner Marketing:** 1,000 signed needs 3,000 to 4,000 partner leads.
- **Partner Field:** AEs want live customers and co-sold pipeline, not partner counts.

## Think again (round 2): will this scale on flat headcount?

- **Finding:** the Foundation data grew the team from 14 to 15; a flat team of 9 replaces it.
- **GTM Operations:** fix the process, then automate it; people act only on cold flags and managed partners.
- **Forward-Deployed AI:** builds 3 and 4 become the operating model: Support Hub answers, agentic PDR drafts and scores, triage clears clean registrations; a person approves every send.
- **GTM Analytics:** measure scale: customers live per team member; share of partner work with no human touch.
- **Finance:** costs grow only with performance, so cost per customer live falls; time saved is not cash saved.
- **Enablement:** one partner solutions architect reviewing every L2 and L3 is the bottleneck; use the rubric plus sampling and a weekly cap.
- **Partner Field:** keep co-sell gated to Select and above to protect AE time.
- **Partner Marketing:** the directory, self-match and co-marketing templates recruit; automated lead scoring is required.

## Conflicts and tradeoffs

Ambition versus budget; signed volume versus activation quality; a small team's attention versus a large long tail, resolved by automation with human approval (decision 46).
