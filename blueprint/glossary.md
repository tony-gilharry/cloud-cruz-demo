# Glossary

**The words this program uses, in plain terms. Each definition matches the Blueprint document it comes from; decision numbers point to the decision log.**

> All of this is synthetic. Cloud Cruz and its partners are invented.

## The program

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Cloud Cruz** | A fictional AI data platform: "the data and AI platform your apps and agents run on" (decision 13). |
| **Ecosystem Partner Program** | Cloud Cruz's partner program, designed from zero in this case study. |
| **North star** | Customers live through partners: Cloud Cruz customers in production with a partner as the sourcing partner, partner of record or implementer (decision 57). |
| **Customer live** | A customer in production, confirmed by a usage event Cloud Cruz can see, not by a partner's word. |
| **Activated** | A partner with its first live joint customer (decision 43). |
| **Beta** | The 90 days before launch, Jan 1 to Mar 31, 2027, with 25 design partners (decision 15). |
| **Design partner** | One of the 25 beta partners. They keep a permanent "Design partner" label in the directory (decision 98). |
| **Go/no-go check** | The Mar 15, 2027 check on four must-haves before launch. Missing one narrows the launch; it never delays it (decision 95). |
| **Guard metric** | One of two monthly checks that protect the running Services motion. A red month pauses the next opening (decision 100). |
| **Program review** | The Jan 1 and Jul 1 reviews where rules change. Changes are announced 90 days ahead and rules hold 12 months (decision 58). |
| **As-of date** | Mar 31, 2028, the end of Year 1. The data and the Salesforce sample both describe the program on that day (decision 102). |

## Partners

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Services path** | Agencies, consultancies and SIs that build and implement Cloud Cruz for customers. Opens at launch, Apr 1, 2027. |
| **Technology path** | ISVs and technology partners that embed or integrate Cloud Cruz, then resellers. Opens Oct 1, 2027 (decision 97). |
| **Segment** | One of four kinds of services firm, each with its own role (decision 31): Anchor, Scale, Specialist, Builder. |
| **Anchor** | Holding-company data and digital units. Invite-only, white glove, the route to large brands. The source of Elite. |
| **Scale** | The digital arms of large consultancies. The enterprise route. |
| **Specialist** | Independent digital and data agencies. Select and Premier targets. |
| **Builder** | Boutique AI studios and consultancies. The beta cohort and the volume. |
| **Managed partner** | One of the 80 partners a partner account manager works directly (decision 101). |
| **Long tail** | The roughly 920 other partners. Automation does the routine work; a person steps in when health turns cold or hot (decision 46). |
| **Partner directory** | The public list of partners on the Cloud Cruz site, where customers can find and match with one (decision 51). |

## Tiers

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Member** | The free entry tier: an agreement and a named contact. 10% commission. |
| **Select** | 4 certified people, 3 live joint customers, 1 public reference. 15% commission, and co-selling with account executives. |
| **Premier** | 10 certified people (3 at Level 2), 10 live customers, 3 references, $175K partner-sourced revenue. 20% commission and the co-marketing fund. |
| **Elite** | By invitation from the Anchor list: 25 certified people (8 at Level 3), 25 live customers, 6 references, $500K, and a joint business plan. 25% commission. |
| **Scorecard** | A partner's live criteria against actuals. The partner sees the same numbers Cloud Cruz sees (decision 33). |
| **Promotion and demotion** | Promotions are checked on the 1st of each month. Demotions happen only at the Jul 1 review, with 90 days' notice (decisions 92, 109). |

## The lifecycle

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Stage** | One of eight steps every partner moves through, each with one owner, a clock and one number: 0 Develop, 1 Acquire, 2 Onboard, 3 Activate, 4 Enable, 5 Co-sell, 6 Maintain, 7 Optimize. |
| **Onboarded** | All six items done: three named contacts, payout details, Slack, a sandbox, both accreditation badges, and a kickoff (decision 40). |
| **Health** | Four questions, each green, yellow or red: are they selling, are their customers winning, are their people skilled, is their delivery clean. Healthy means no red (decision 54). |
| **Activation rating** | Cold, warm or hot, from the time between a partner's first registration, first win and first live customer (decision 43). |
| **Touch cadence** | How often each segment gets a business review, from monthly for Anchors to an automated digest for Builders (decision 55). |

## Scores and routing

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Gates** | Two must-haves before a firm is scored: an AI or data delivery practice, and three or more mid-market B2B software clients. |
| **Fit score** | 100 points for who the partner is. 70 or more is approved; 50 to 69 goes to a partner manager; under 50 goes to nurture (decisions 29, 37). |
| **Intent score** | 100 points for how ready the partner is now. Signals older than 30 days count half (decision 68). |
| **Deal in hand** | A partner that arrives with a real customer deal. Always urgent: a Slack alert, and the team engages that day (decision 35). |
| **Temperature** | Nurture, warm, hot or urgent, set by fit times intent. Each partner development rep gets the top 8 leads a day (decision 70). |
| **Typed-decision layer** | A model returns a typed answer (a choice, score or yes/no) with a probability, and Cloud Cruz's own weights turn it into points. Low-confidence answers go to a person (decision 69). |

## Money

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Commission** | A share of collected revenue on a deal the partner sourced: 10% to 25% by tier. Earned when the customer's invoice is paid (decisions 21, 72). |
| **Hold** | Every commission waits 60 days after the invoice is paid before it can be released (decision 21). |
| **Accrual** | The ledger record of one commission: Earned, Held, Released or Reversed. It lives in Salesforce (decision 24). |
| **Rate lock** | The rate at close holds for that deal's whole term; a demotion never cuts pay on customers already landed (decision 73). |
| **SPIFF** | A one-time bonus for a milestone: $500 for a first live customer, $2,500 for a first Enterprise go-live, $1,000 for each customer retained 12 months (decision 76). |
| **Accelerator** | A higher rate above a threshold. The mechanism exists; the numbers are still open (decision 76). |
| **Co-marketing fund** | Money for Premier and Elite campaigns of $3,500, $5,000 or $10,000, paid against proof of performance. Never taken out of commissions (decision 77). |
| **Implementation package** | Cloud Cruz pays for a partner's first hours on an Enterprise deal: $1,750, $3,500 or $7,000 by deal size. Needs a Level 2 technical person on the deal (decisions 45, 61, 91). |
| **Contribution points** | Points for community answers, posts and articles. They raise a partner's rank in the directory, and Select and above need a minimum (decision 78). |

## Deals

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Deal registration** | A partner's claim on a customer deal. Cloud Cruz answers within 2 business days, and approval protects the partner for 90 days (decision 79). |
| **Sourced** | The partner brought the deal. Only a sourcing partner, or a partner of record, earns commission. |
| **Influenced** | The partner helped win the deal. It counts toward tier but pays no cash; it earns first offer on the implementation (decision 74). |
| **Implementer** | The partner that delivers the customer's implementation. |
| **Partner of record** | The partner named on a cloud marketplace deal, paid 5% (decision 72). |
| **Co-sell** | The partner and a Cloud Cruz account executive working a deal together. Select and above (decision 49). |
| **Rules of engagement** | The AE owns the customer; the partner account manager owns the partner. The AE keeps full quota credit on partner deals (decision 50). |
| **Self-serve, Premium, Enterprise** | Cloud Cruz's plans: under $12K a year, $12K to $50K ACV, and $50K ACV and up. |

## Credentials

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Accreditation** | The Sales and Technical badges earned during onboarding. They don't expire (decision 80). |
| **Level 1: Foundations** | An exam, a sandbox lab and a Problem Statement Canvas for a real target customer. |
| **Level 2: Delivery** | Two customer go-lives, or two signed business cases with a baseline and target ROI. |
| **Level 3: Advisor** | Three Readiness Reviews run, or three accounts expanded on proven ROI. |
| **Certified person** | Someone with an active credential and hands-on product use in the last 90 days. Only they count toward a tier (decision 47). |
| **Readiness Review** | A one-page review of a customer's setup on four pillars: Trust, AI quality, Cost, Adoption (decision 64). |
| **Problem Statement Canvas** | A public framework (Marius Ursache, 2018) for describing a customer's problem: context, customer type, root problem, impact and alternatives. |

## People

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Head of partner ecosystem** | Owns Optimize, the long tail, the program reviews, the launch checks and the guard metrics. |
| **PDR** | Partner development rep. Finds firms that fit and owns each lead through signing. |
| **Partner manager** | Runs onboarding, decides admissions at fit 50 to 69, and works the long tail by exception. |
| **Partner account manager** | Works the 80 managed partners from Activate through Maintain, and co-sells with the AEs. |
| **PSA** | Partner solutions architect. Leads up to 12 open package deals at once, largest first (decision 99). |
| **AE** | Account executive, Cloud Cruz's salesperson who owns the customer. |

## Systems

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Salesforce** | The one source of truth: partners, deals, registrations, tiers, health and the commission ledger. |
| **introw** | The partner portal (PRM): courses, deal registration, co-marketing and commission statements. |
| **Tipalti** | Pays released commissions and checks each partner's identity and tax details (KYC). |
| **Credly** | Issues credential badges to the wallet partners already use. |
| **Crossbeam** | Shows which accounts Cloud Cruz and a partner share, to find co-sell targets and avoid collisions. |
| **Support Hub** | Version-controlled program docs, answered with citations by the AI Answer Engine. What it can't answer becomes a ticket. |
| **Slack Connect** | A shared Slack channel between Cloud Cruz and one partner: design partners in the beta, Premier and Elite after launch. |
